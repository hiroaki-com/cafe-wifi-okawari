Before you use it

cafe-wifi-okawari is an unofficial tool that accepts the terms again and reconnects automatically when the free Wi‑Fi at a café times out. It is not endorsed or recommended by Wi2, USEN or any shop, and the author has not obtained the providers' permission for accepting the terms automatically.

  • The first time, read the terms on the login page and accept them yourself. At each later time-out, the tool sends the acceptance again without showing the terms or any notices. Use it only if you understand this and want the terms accepted again automatically.
  • Use it only as far as the provider's terms and the shop's rules allow. If it is unclear whether automated use is allowed, connect the usual way until you can confirm with the provider. Do not use it to get around usage limits or required steps.
  • How it judges your acceptance, identifies networks and detects changes to the terms has limits. It does not guarantee that you accepted the terms yourself at each shop.
  • Stop using it if the terms change, a usage limit or suspension is shown, you are asked to stop, or it keeps failing. To stop and remove it, choose "Uninstall…" from the coffee cup in the menu bar.

Full text: "Usage notes and automatic acceptance"
https://github.com/hiroaki-com/cafe-wifi-okawari/blob/main/USAGE-NOTES.en.md

What gets installed

It is installed for this user only (in ~/.local/bin in your home folder, and as your LaunchAgents). The tool's code never runs with administrator (root) privileges, either while installing or while running in the background. It talks only to Apple's connectivity check page and the Wi‑Fi login service, and sends nothing to the author's servers.

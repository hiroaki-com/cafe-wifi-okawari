Installed

A coffee cup icon appears in the menu bar.

  1. On a shop's Wi‑Fi, accept the terms yourself on the login page the first time only.
  2. At each later time-out, the tool accepts them again automatically.

Check the status from the cup's menu. To remove the tool, choose "Uninstall…" from the cup's menu.

How to use it: https://github.com/hiroaki-com/cafe-wifi-okawari/blob/main/README.en.md
